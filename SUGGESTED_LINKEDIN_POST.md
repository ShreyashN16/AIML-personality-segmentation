# Suggested LinkedIn Post

**Headline:** I built an AI that ruthlessly explains why you're single 💔🤖

**Body:**

"Why are you still single?" is the worst question to hear at family dinners.
So I built an AI to answer it for me.

🚀 Introducing **"Why Are You Still Single? - 3D AI Personality Experience"**

I didn't want to build just another form. I wanted an **Experience**.
So I combined 3D web graphics, rigorous Machine Learning, and a neon-soaked UI.

**🛠 The Tech Stack:**
• **Backend:** FastAPI & Python (handling the logic)
• **ML:** XGBoost & Scikit-Learn (trained on 2,000+ behavioral datapoints)
• **XAI:** SHAP (to explain *exactly* which red flag is your biggest problem)
• **Frontend:** React, Tailwind, & Three.js (for that floating 3D goodness)

**💡 How it works:**
1. You answer 10 vague questions about your dating habits.
2. The ML model predicts your "Single Persona" (e.g., *The Overthinker*, *Career Obsessed*).
3. It generates a dynamic Radar Chart and gives you actionable (and slightly mean) advice.

This project taught me how to bridge the gap between "Data Science" and "Product Design". ML doesn't have to be boring spreadsheets. It can be immersive, fun, and a little bit savage.
